# BS vinařské potřeby – what is worth reading and what the app took from it

BS vinařské potřeby (Velké Bílovice, Mikulov, Znojmo; vinarskepotreby.cz) publish about 700 advisory articles. Their photos and product lists are copyrighted, so the app links to them instead of copying; the knowledge below was folded into the app on 6 September 2026 (spray-programme advice and product keywords, cellar protocol template "BS", soil reference values, links from the Overview and the spray programme).

## Weekly series in season

- **Situační zpráva o výskytu patogenů révy vinné** (`/clanky/detail/ochrana-revy-vinne-NN-tyden…`, PDF): vegetation state with BBCH and variety photos, protection measures, per-station infection pressure (Šteberla for downy mildew, Kast for powdery mildew, botrytis), product traffic light with PHI, 7-day outlook. Linked from *Spray programme → External sources*.
- **Předsběrová analýza** (`predsberova-analyza-<year>-NN-tyden`): weekly must analyses (glucose+fructose, potential alcohol, titratable acidity, pH, YAN) of the main varieties in Mikulov, Slovácko, Velké Pavlovice, Znojmo and Malé Karpaty. Sampling advice: about 0.5 kg berries, from two rows alternating shaded and sunny sides, every fifth vine, skip the first 10 m and sick vines, roughly 10 berries per vine from top/bottom and inside/outside of several bunches. Linked from *Overview → Ripening*.

## Spray plans and protection

- `postrikovy-plan-vinice-2025` (A3 leaflet, PDF): 10 growth stages (bud break, 5 leaves, 9 leaves, before flowering, after flowering, shot-sized berries, pea-sized berries, start and end of bunch closure, colouring) × four plans (profi, hobby, profi eco, hobby eco) with rows for downy mildew, powdery mildew, botrytis, overwintering pests, moths, mites, weeds and foliar nutrition. The hobby and hobby-eco rows are shown per window in the app's spray programme as "BS spray plan 2025" with the leaflet's general rules (sulphur in every mix, Cu ≤ 4 kg/ha/year in organic, no mixing of concentrates, fosetyl-Al incompatibilities, Karathane New on visible oidium max 4×). Page 1 of the leaflet is a photo sheet of diseases, pests and nutrient deficiencies (N, P, K, Mg, S, Fe) – the best Czech reference photos for deficiencies, but copyrighted.
- `doporucene-pripravky-na-ochranu-revy-pro-hobby-pestitele`: hobby doses per 10 l – downy mildew Acrobat MZ WG 25 g, Cabrio Top 20 g, Ridomil Gold MZ Pepite 25 g, Pergado F 25 g; powdery mildew Topas 100 EC 3 ml, IQ Crystal 1.5 ml, Talent 1.8 ml, Dynali 6.5 ml, Kumulus WG 30 g, Sulfomax 30 ml; foliar Wuxal Super 30 ml, Wuxal Kalcium 40–50 ml, Brexil Fe 20–25 g, Vínofit 10 ml. Systemic products before flowering.
- `moznost-poslednich-aplikaci-postriku-na-vinicich`: late applications – Cymbal (cymoxanil) as a downy-mildew "extinguisher"; botrytis PHIs Teldor 14, Prolectus 14, Magnicur Quick 14, Pyrus 21, Scala 28, Switch 35, Vitisan short; fungicides only work on green leaf.
- `citlivost-revy-vinne-k-houbovym-chorobam-v-obdobi-kveteni-revy`: inflorescences susceptible to powdery mildew from about a week before flowering (BBCH 55), berries until ~2 mm (BBCH 73); systemic fungicides around flowering, rotate chemical classes, contacts in between.
- `pozor-na-primarni-infekci-peronospory`, `nepodcenujte-prvni-postrik-sirou`, `bs-doporuceni-pro-prvni-jarni-postrik`, `antirezistentni-strategie-v-ochrane-proti-padli-revy`, `vhodny-termin-pro-odlisteni-zony-hroznu`, `zacina-zamekani-hroznu`, `vinice-po-poskozeni-kroupami`, `po-kroupach-je-dulezita-vyziva-nikoliv-ochrana`.

## Cellar technology per variety

`sauvignon`, `palava`, `modry-portugal`, `muller-thurgau`, `veltlinske-zelene`, `tramin-cerveny`, `ryzlink-rynsky`, `neuburske`, `hibernal`, `muskat-moravsky`, `frankovka`, `cabernet-sauvignon`, `technologie-vyroby-vina-pro-merlot`, `technologie-vyroby-vina-pro-rulandske-modre` (no sheet for Rulandské bílé / šedé). Pattern used in the "BS protocol" template: SO₂ + ascorbic acid + gallic tannin on the grapes (Assotan 20 g/hl white, 10 g/hl red), aroma or colour enzyme, protein fining of the must (Plantis / must gelatine) with a settling enzyme, selected yeast 20 g/hl, nutrients in three steps (Nutriferm Arom Plus 20 g/hl at pitching, Advance/Vit 10–20 g/hl mid-way, No Stop 20–30 g/hl in the last third), thiol protection for tropical Sauvignon (Prolie FT 30 g/hl), oak alternatives for Pálava and Modrý Portugal, MLF bacteria (Viniflora Oenos) for reds.

Also: `optimalizace-vyzivy-kvasinek-behem-alkoholove-fermentace`, `oxid-siricity-ve-vine`, `malolakticka-fermentace`, `rizika-vysokeho-ph-mostu`, `nejcastejsi-technologicke-chyby-pri-osetrovani-a-stabilizaci-vin`, `jak-na-stabilizaci-krystalickych-zakalu`, `doporuceni-bs-enologa-pred-vinobranim`, `enolog-doporucuje-pripravky-pro-vinobrani-2026`.

## Soil, nutrition, laboratory

- `pudni-rozbor-a-vyznam-zivin`: vineyard pH 6.5–7.5, humus 2–3 %, K:Mg about 2:1, active lime for rootstock choice; sample 0–30 and 30–60 cm separately, 1–1.5 kg from several spots (now in the Overview soil table hint).
- `listova-analyza-a-zjisteni-vyzivoveho-stavu-revy`, `optimalni-termin-pro-listovou-analyzu`, `vapnik-a-chloroza-na-vinicich`, `dulezitost-draselnych-hnojiv-v-obdobi-sucha`, `jarni-zasobni-hnojeni`, `podzimni-prihnojeni-vinic-a-sadu`, `zelene-hnojeni-do-vinic`.
- `analyza-mostu-organicke-kyseliny`: laboratory services S0149 (grapes, 0.5 kg), S0150 / S0153 (must, 0.2 l, with organic acids).

## Photos

BS photos (varieties at veraison, disease symptoms, deficiencies) are theirs; none are in the repository. The photo pages of the older advisory articles (`/out/pictures/wysiwigpro/...`) are no longer online (404 as of September 2026), so linking to them is not an option either. What remains is page 1 of the 2025 leaflet: `tools/bs_leaflet_tiles.py` cuts it into per-topic photos for the git-ignored `assets/guide-extra/` folder, shown in the guide with the credit line – a private copy for your own phone, not for distribution. The field guide keeps Wikimedia Commons images; for nutrient deficiencies, where Commons has nothing, the BS articles `vapnik-a-chloroza-na-vinicich`, `aktualni-vyzivove-problemy-ve-vinicich` and `cerne-listy-u-revy-vinne` are good references to open from the guide.
